## Structure (H2/H3 outline)
1. The uncomfortable truth: 8-15% check-average drops documented in fine dining/full-service venues that went QR-only
2. Why it happens: guests scroll to what they want on a phone and skip dessert/cocktail sections they'd have seen flipping a physical menu
3. Where QR-only works great: fast-casual/QSR (15-30% faster table turns, no guidance-moment lost because there wasn't one)
4. Where QR-only is risky: fine dining and full-service (the "$3-8K in print savings" gets dwarfed by lost check average — show the math)
5. The recommended hybrid: QR-first + a small stack of paper menus at the host stand on request (saves ~85-90% of print budget without the revenue hit)
6. A short decision checklist: "should your restaurant go QR-only, hybrid, or QR-assist?"
